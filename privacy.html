Our promise: We never sell your personal data to third parties. Your information is used only to process your orders and improve your shopping experience.

01 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Genex - Global Xperience ("Genex", "we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information about visitors and customers of our website (genex.lk) and those who contact us via WhatsApp, email, or in person.

By using our website or placing an order with us, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you disagree, please refrain from using our services.

We are based in Kamburupitiya, Matara District, Sri Lanka, and operate under Sri Lankan laws and regulations.

02 Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Name - when registering an account or placing an order
  • Email address - for account registration, order confirmation and communication
  • Phone number - for delivery coordination and WhatsApp communication
  • Delivery address - for order delivery
  • Messages and inquiries - sent through our contact form or WhatsApp

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser and device data - browser type, operating system, screen size
  • Usage data - pages visited, time spent, links clicked
  • IP address - for security purposes

Information stored locally on your device:

  • Shopping cart contents (stored in browser localStorage)
  • Wishlist items (stored in browser localStorage)
  • Account login status (stored in browser localStorage)

We do not collect or store credit card numbers, bank account details or any financial payment information through our website.

03 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Communicating with you about your order status, delivery and inquiries
  • Sending you important account-related notifications
  • Responding to your questions and support requests
  • Improving our website, products and service quality
  • Sending promotional offers and newsletters (only if you have subscribed)
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring security

We will only use your information for the purposes stated above and will not use it in any way that you would not reasonably expect.

04 Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Courier partners - We share your name, phone number and delivery address with our courier partners solely to fulfil your delivery.
  • Legal requirements - We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, a court order or a government authority.
  • Business protection - To investigate or prevent fraud, security threats or violations of our policies.

Any third parties we work with are required to handle your data responsibly and only for the purpose it was shared.

05 Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses browser localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store your cart, wishlist and account session data. This data is stored entirely on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

What is stored in localStorage:

  • genex_cart - your shopping cart items
  • genex_wishlist - your saved wishlist items
  • genex_user - your current login session
  • genex_users - registered account data

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage in your browser settings, or by logging out of your account.

We may also use third-party services (such as Google Fonts and Font Awesome) that may set their own cookies. These are standard web services and do not track your shopping behaviour on our site.

06 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

However, please be aware that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Account passwords stored on your device are never transmitted to our servers. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your account and not sharing it with anyone.

07 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access - request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction - request that we update inaccurate information
  • Deletion - request that we delete your personal information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Unsubscribe - opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 09. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

08 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website after changes are made constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
