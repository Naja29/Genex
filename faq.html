Delivery & Shipping

Yes, we deliver to all districts across Sri Lanka through trusted courier partners including Pronto, Kapruka and other courier services. Delivery is available to both urban and rural addresses.

Standard delivery timeframes after order confirmation:

  • Southern Province (Matara, Galle, Hambantota): Same day or next day
  • Western Province (Colombo, Gampaha, Kalutara): 1-2 business days
  • All other provinces: 2-3 business days

Delivery times may vary during public holidays or bad weather conditions.

Delivery charges are calculated based on weight, dimensions and destination. Typical charges:

  • Small items (e.g., RAM, accessories): Rs. 300 – Rs. 500
  • Medium items (e.g., hard drives, keyboards): Rs. 500 – Rs. 800
  • Large items (e.g., monitors, PC cases): Rs. 800 – Rs. 1,500

We offer free delivery on orders over Rs. 50,000. Contact us for an exact quote on your order.

Once your order is dispatched, we will send you a tracking number via WhatsApp. You can use this tracking number on the respective courier's website to track your delivery in real time. If you have any issues, contact us directly and we will assist you.

If you are not available at the time of delivery, the courier will typically attempt re-delivery or hold the parcel at a nearby depot. They will contact you via the phone number provided. If the parcel is not collected within the specified time, it will be returned to us. Please contact us immediately to arrange re-delivery, which may incur an additional charge.

Returns & Warranty

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for items that are defective, damaged, or not as described. Items must be in their original, unused condition with all original packaging, accessories and documentation intact.

For full details, please read our Return Policy.

Contact us immediately via WhatsApp (+94 77 723 7962) or email (genecoretech@gmail.com) with:

  • Your order details / invoice number
  • A clear description of the fault
  • Photos or a short video showing the issue

We will assess the issue and arrange a replacement, repair under warranty, or refund as appropriate.

We do not accept returns for change-of-mind purchases unless the product is unused, sealed in its original packaging, and returned within 7 days. A restocking fee may apply. Please contact us before returning any item.

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. Refunds are issued via bank transfer or the original payment method. You will be notified via WhatsApp when the refund is processed.
