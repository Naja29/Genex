Our Story

Built on Trust & Quality

Genex - Global Xperience was founded with a simple mission: to provide Sri Lanka with access to genuine, high-quality computer parts and electronics at the best possible prices.

Based in Kamburupitiya, we started as a small retail outlet and grew into a trusted wholesale and retail supplier, serving customers and businesses across the entire island.

We stock the latest processors, graphics cards, RAM, storage, monitors, peripherals and mobile accessories from the world's leading brands - all backed by manufacturer warranties.

100% Genuine products with manufacturer warranty
Wholesale & Retail pricing for all customers
Island-wide delivery within 24 hours
